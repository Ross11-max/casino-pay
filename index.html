Do it by hand in Trust Wallet

  2. Pick the one marked BNB Smart Chain (small BNB badge on the icon), not Ethereum, Tron or opBNB.

Pay from an exchange app

Binance
  1. Tap Assets → Withdraw → USDT → Send via Crypto Network.
  2. Paste the address (tap Copy above).
  3. Network: choose BNB Smart Chain (BEP20).
  4. Enter the amount so the store receives USDT (see the note below), then confirm.
Coinbase
  1. Tap Send (or Transfer → Send crypto) and pick USDT.
  2. Paste the address.
  3. Network: choose BNB Chain / BNB Smart Chain. If it isn't offered, use another app; don't pick Ethereum.
  4. Enter USDT plus any fee and send.
Any other exchange or wallet
  1. Withdraw / send USDT.
  2. Paste the address.
  3. Network: BNB Smart Chain, also called BEP20 or BSC.
  4. Make sure the store receives USDT.

Exchange fees: most exchanges take their fee out of what you send. If the app shows "You will receive", that number must be at least USDT.
